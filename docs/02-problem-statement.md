# Problem Statement

## Background

Artificial Intelligence has become a part of daily work. Developers, students, researchers, and businesses rely on AI tools to improve productivity.

However, users still depend on multiple applications for different tasks.

---

## Current Problems

### 1. Multiple AI Platforms

Users switch between ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, and other AI tools depending on the task.

This causes:
- Context switching
- Wasted time
- Duplicate conversations

---

### 2. Documents Are Separate

Documents are stored in Google Drive or local folders while AI conversations happen elsewhere.

There is no unified workspace where AI understands uploaded knowledge.

---

### 3. No Persistent Workspace

Most AI chats are temporary.

Users cannot easily organize:

- Projects
- Notes
- Research
- Documents
- Conversations

inside one structured workspace.

---

### 4. Team Collaboration

Modern teams use multiple applications:

- Slack
- Notion
- Google Docs
- ChatGPT

Knowledge becomes scattered across different platforms.

---

## Existing Solutions

Current platforms solve only part of the problem.

| Product | Strength | Limitation |
|---------|----------|------------|
| ChatGPT | Excellent AI Chat | Limited workspace organization |
| Claude | Strong document understanding | Limited collaboration |
| Gemini | Google ecosystem integration | Not focused on project management |
| Notion AI | Great knowledge management | AI capabilities are limited |
| Perplexity | Excellent research | Not a complete workspace |

---

## Proposed Solution

Enterprise AI Workspace combines:

- AI Conversations
- Workspace Management
- Document Intelligence
- Multiple AI Models
- Team Collaboration

inside one modern application.

---

## Expected Impact

Users can manage their work from a single platform without constantly switching between multiple applications.

The platform improves productivity, organization, and collaboration while providing access to multiple AI models.
